# S10 · Measuring AI Ability to Complete Long Software Tasks

**Source:** Kwa and colleagues, METR · 2025; arXiv v4 2026-07-10
**Evidence type:** empirical benchmark study
**Accessed:** 2026-11-01 · [Original source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14499v4)

## What the source supports

The task-completion horizon expresses task difficulty in human-expert time at a specified model success probability. The paper studies trends on its software-task suite.

## Boundary of the evidence

A 50% horizon is not a deployment reliability target, an agent's elapsed runtime, or proof of general labor replacement. Historical fitted trends do not guarantee future doubling.

## Use in this study

Forecast capability separately from reliable deployment. The paper proposes measuring recovery, authorization violations and human supervision alongside completion.

This note is an editorial evidence assessment, not an independent replication or an endorsement by the cited authors.
