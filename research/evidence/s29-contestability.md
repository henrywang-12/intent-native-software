# S29 · About the Digital Markets Act

**Source:** European Commission · accessed 2026-11-01
**Evidence type:** official regulatory overview
**Accessed:** 2026-11-01 · [Original source](https://digital-markets-act.ec.europa.eu/about-dma_en)

## What the source supports

The Commission describes a framework aimed at fairer, more contestable digital markets, with obligations for designated gatekeepers in covered services.

## Boundary of the evidence

This study does not determine any company's legal designation or give legal advice. An agent product's eventual treatment depends on facts and applicable rules.

## Use in this study

A strategy assuming permanent unrestricted gatekeeping omits an important constraint. Portability and transparent routing can also support trust and provider participation.
