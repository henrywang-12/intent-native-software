# S25 · Not what you've signed up for: Compromising Real-World LLM-Integrated Applications with Indirect Prompt Injection

**Source:** Greshake and colleagues · 2023
**Evidence type:** security research paper
**Accessed:** 2026-11-01 · [Original source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.12173)

## What the source supports

The authors show how attacker-controlled retrieved content can be treated as instructions by integrated language-model systems.

## Boundary of the evidence

The historical demonstrations do not establish the vulnerability of a particular current product. The architectural issue remains relevant wherever untrusted data can influence tools.

## Use in this study

A provider description is evidence about an offer, not authority to change the user's goal, export data or choose a payment recipient. Test those boundaries directly.

This note is an editorial evidence assessment, not an independent replication or an endorsement by the cited authors.
