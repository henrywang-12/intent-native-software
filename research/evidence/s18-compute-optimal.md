# S18 · Training Compute-Optimal Large Language Models

**Source:** Hoffmann and colleagues · 2022-03-29
**Evidence type:** scaling research paper
**Accessed:** 2026-11-01 · [Original source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.15556)

## What the source supports

The study examines allocation of training compute between parameter count and training tokens, challenging parameter count alone as a measure of an efficient model.

## Boundary of the evidence

Training-compute optimization is not total deployment-cost optimization. Inference demand, latency, hardware utilization and serving constraints can change the preferred design.

## Use in this study

Model forecasts should specify which budget is constrained. A smaller model can be economically attractive without being the strongest model on every evaluation.

This note is an editorial evidence assessment, not an independent replication or an endorsement by the cited authors.
