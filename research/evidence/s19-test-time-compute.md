# S19 · Scaling LLM Test-Time Compute Optimally can be More Effective than Scaling Model Parameters

**Source:** Snell, Lee, Xu and Kumar · 2024-08-06; v1 used
**Evidence type:** inference-scaling research paper
**Accessed:** 2026-11-01 · [Original source](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.03314v1)

## What the source supports

The study compares verification/search and revision strategies, finding that task difficulty affects efficient inference allocation. Its search analysis also documents verifier over-optimization.

## Boundary of the evidence

More inference is not a universal guarantee of correctness. Results depend on tasks, model and verifier; mathematical-answer verification is not proof that a real booking exists.

## Use in this study

Spend compute where it changes decisions. Separate reasoning verification from authoritative evidence of external effects, and test escalating compute against a fixed-budget baseline.

This note is an editorial evidence assessment, not an independent replication or an endorsement by the cited authors.
