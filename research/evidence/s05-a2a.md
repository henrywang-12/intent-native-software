# S05 · Announcing the Agent2Agent Protocol

**Source:** Google Developers · 2025-04-09
**Evidence type:** protocol launch
**Accessed:** 2026-11-01 · [Original source](https://developers.googleblog.com/en/a2a-a-new-era-of-agent-interoperability/)

## What the source supports

A2A was introduced for communication and task coordination between independently built agents, complementing tool/context connections.

## Boundary of the evidence

Interoperable messages do not by themselves align business incentives, transfer a user's legal authority, or settle disputes. The launch's stated design goals are not a security audit.

## Use in this study

Separate transport interoperability from semantic, authorization and commercial interoperability. Each layer can become a different competitive bottleneck.

This note is an editorial evidence assessment, not an independent replication or an endorsement by the cited authors.
