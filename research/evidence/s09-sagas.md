# S09 · Sagas

**Source:** Hector Garcia-Molina and Kenneth Salem · 1987
**Evidence type:** database research paper
**Accessed:** 2026-11-01 · [Original source](https://sigmodrecord.org/1987/12/09/sagas/)

## What the source supports

The saga approach decomposes a long transaction into smaller transactions with compensating actions when needed.

## Boundary of the evidence

Business compensation need not erase the original effect: a refund can leave fees, information disclosure or inconvenience. The public index and linked paper are the reference, not a novel claim of this study.

## Use in this study

A travel workflow should distinguish cancellation, compensation and reconciliation. A proposal to recover from failure must specify who authorizes the compensating action and which harms remain.

This note is an editorial evidence assessment, not an independent replication or an endorsement by the cited authors.
