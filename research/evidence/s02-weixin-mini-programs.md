# S02 · Weixin Mini Programs

**Source:** Tencent · undated; accessed 2026-11-01
**Evidence type:** company product description
**Accessed:** 2026-11-01 · [Original source](https://www.tencent.com/products/weixin-mini-programs/)

## What the source supports

Tencent describes services accessible within Weixin through search and QR codes, spanning merchants and public services.

## Boundary of the evidence

A shared distribution surface does not establish unrestricted agent access, shared authorization for every service, or an independently measured agent-success advantage.

## Use in this study

The relevant architectural precedent is colocated discovery and service access. Our hypothesis is that fewer organizational handoffs can reduce execution friction; that needs a matched-task experiment.

This note is an editorial evidence assessment, not an independent replication or an endorsement by the cited authors.
