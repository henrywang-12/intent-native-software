# S22 · Peter Thiel's Quest for One-of-a-Kind Ideas

**Source:** Stanford Law School event report · 2014
**Evidence type:** first-party event report
**Accessed:** 2026-11-01 · [Original source](https://law.stanford.edu/press/peter-thiels-quest-for-one-of-a-kind-ideas/)

## What the source supports

The report connects Thiel's Zero to One argument to differentiated companies and his preference for monopoly over undifferentiated competition.

## Boundary of the evidence

This is an account of a strategic argument, not empirical proof that any early platform becomes a monopoly or that monopoly benefits users.

## Use in this study

Translate the idea into a testable wedge: solve a narrow coordination problem substantially better, then test whether adjacent expansion improves economics without eroding service quality.
