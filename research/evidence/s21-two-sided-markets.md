# S21 · Platform Competition in Two-Sided Markets

**Source:** Jean-Charles Rochet and Jean Tirole · 2003
**Evidence type:** economic theory paper
**Accessed:** 2026-11-01 · [Original source](https://academic.oup.com/jeea/article-abstract/1/4/990/2280902)

## What the source supports

The framework studies how platforms attract both sides and how the allocation of prices affects participation and surplus.

## Boundary of the evidence

The publication abstract supports the framing; this paper does not reproduce or estimate the original model. Cross-side effects do not alone imply a unique winner.

## Use in this study

For an agent marketplace, distinguish users, service providers and agent developers. Subsidizing one group can help participation while still producing unsustainable economics.

This note is an editorial evidence assessment, not an independent replication or an endorsement by the cited authors.
