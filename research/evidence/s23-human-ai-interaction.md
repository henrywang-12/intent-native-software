# S23 · Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction

**Source:** Amershi and colleagues; Microsoft Research · 2019
**Evidence type:** researcher-authored study overview
**Accessed:** 2026-11-01 · [Original source](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/articles/guidelines-for-human-ai-interaction-eighteen-best-practices-for-human-centered-ai-design/)

## What the source supports

The guidelines address capability expectations, interaction, errors and adaptation over time, based on a synthesized and evaluated set of human-AI design practices.

## Boundary of the evidence

Guidelines are not evidence that a particular approval UI produces informed consent. Accessibility, comprehension and recovery must be tested with the target population.

## Use in this study

Preserve inspectable state, correction and stable manual controls alongside conversation. The proposed human studies test comprehension rather than counting approval clicks.

This note is an editorial evidence assessment, not an independent replication or an endorsement by the cited authors.
