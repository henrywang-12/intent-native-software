# S11 · Measuring the Impact of Early-2025 AI on Experienced Open-Source Developer Productivity

**Source:** Becker, Rush, Barnes and Rein, METR · 2025-07
**Evidence type:** randomized controlled study
**Accessed:** 2026-11-01 · [Original source](https://metr.org/Early_2025_AI_Experienced_OS_Devs_Study-paper.pdf)

## What the source supports

In a study of 16 developers completing 246 tasks in familiar repositories, early-2025 AI access increased completion time by 19%, despite participants expecting a speedup.

## Boundary of the evidence

This setting, sample and tool generation do not identify the effect on novices, new projects or future tools. Self-reported ease and measured speed are different outcomes.

## Use in this study

Count review and integration time in software economics. A code-generation demo alone cannot establish a reduction in the cost of a maintained service.

This note is an editorial evidence assessment, not an independent replication or an endorsement by the cited authors.
