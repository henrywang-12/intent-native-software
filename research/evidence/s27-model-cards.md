# S27 · Model Cards for Model Reporting

**Source:** Mitchell and colleagues · 2019; arXiv v2
**Evidence type:** reporting framework paper
**Accessed:** 2026-11-01 · [Original source](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.03993v2)

## What the source supports

Model cards document intended use, evaluation conditions and performance characteristics, including relevant subgroup differences.

## Boundary of the evidence

Documentation does not itself establish quality. An unmeasured field should remain unmeasured rather than being filled with a marketing promise.

## Use in this study

The release includes a methods record, evidence boundaries and proposed evaluation protocols. Every forecast identifies how it could be resolved or withdrawn.
