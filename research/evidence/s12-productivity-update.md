# S12 · We are Changing our Developer Productivity Experiment Design

**Source:** METR · 2026-02-24
**Evidence type:** research methods update
**Accessed:** 2026-11-01 · [Original source](https://metr.org/blog/2026-02-24-uplift-update/)

## What the source supports

METR reports that selection effects and measurement difficulties weaken interpretation of its later productivity experiment, while describing reasons to expect more benefit from newer tools.

## Boundary of the evidence

The update is neither confirmation of a universal slowdown nor a clean estimate of a universal speedup. Opting out of the no-AI condition changes which tasks and people remain observable.

## Use in this study

Evaluation must record refusals, attrition and concurrent-agent time. The study's maintenance protocol includes those denominators rather than reporting only successful completions.

This note is an editorial evidence assessment, not an independent replication or an endorsement by the cited authors.
