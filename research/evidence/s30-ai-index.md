# S30 · AI Index 2026: Technical Performance

**Source:** Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI · 2026
**Evidence type:** research synthesis
**Accessed:** 2026-11-01 · [Original source](https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report/technical-performance)

## What the source supports

The report describes rapid benchmark progress and variation in open-versus-closed model gaps. It presents multiple measurements rather than a single universal capability scale.

## Boundary of the evidence

Aggregate leaderboard summaries do not measure a particular enterprise workflow or predict permanent leadership. This study makes no latest-model ranking claim.

## Use in this study

Forecast a changing cost-quality frontier and preserve model substitution. Market power may remain at distribution or service access even when model competition is vigorous.
