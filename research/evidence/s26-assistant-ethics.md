# S26 · The Ethics of Advanced AI Assistants

**Source:** Gabriel and colleagues · 2024-04; v2
**Evidence type:** conceptual research paper
**Accessed:** 2026-11-01 · [Original source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.16244v2)

## What the source supports

The paper considers assistants that plan and act across domains, including relationships with users, manipulation, privacy, cooperation and societal effects.

## Boundary of the evidence

The abstract-level mapping used here is not an empirical estimate of harm or a claim to resolve the paper's normative questions.

## Use in this study

Coexistence requires more than preserving a human approval step: affected people, providers and non-users also need ways to understand, contest and correct agent-mediated actions.

This note is an editorial evidence assessment, not an independent replication or an endorsement by the cited authors.
