# Methods and evidence boundaries

**Edition:** 2026-11-01. **Type:** AI-assisted position paper and research program, not a peer-reviewed empirical paper.

## Research method

This is a purposive narrative synthesis, not a systematic review or meta-analysis. Searches targeted primary research, official protocol specifications, company product announcements and economic theory relevant to delegated action. Sources were accessed on 2026-11-01. Historical versions are explicitly identified where pinned. Selection was guided by the paper's argument and is subject to selection bias; the register is not an exhaustive inventory of the literature.

The evidence notes distinguish observed or reported findings, interpretation and limits. Company announcements establish what a company announced, not independently measured effectiveness. Standards describe semantics or requirements, not compliance of every implementation. Research abstracts and overviews support only the limited claims recorded in their notes. Full-text sections were additionally inspected for AgentDojo's evaluation construction, DeepSeek-R1's limitations, test-time computation's search analysis, and authorization/idempotence semantics. No proprietary Walify sources were read for this paper.

The Sagas source is linked through its original publication index; its linked full-text download was unavailable during this pass. The note uses the established high-level compensation concept, without reporting numerical results or claiming a new full-text review. Economic-theory claims from S21 are limited to the publication abstract; the accompanying dynamical model is our own unestimated illustration.

## Contribution and conflict

The contribution is a synthesis of execution contracts, human control and marketplace incentives, accompanied by falsifiable protocols and reproducible sensitivity analyses. It does not claim to invent APIs, workflow orchestration, OAuth, compensation or platform economics. The author is Walify's founder and has an interest in the proposed opportunity. Walify's vision is author-stated; the public code's demonstrated scope is separate.

AI tools assisted research organization, drafting, code and review. A fresh AI reader reviewed clarity and unsupported inferences; this is editorial review, not independent peer review or endorsement. No laboratory, researcher, investor or public figure is represented as endorsing this work.

## Reproduction and limits

The five analytical scripts use Python's standard library and perform no network or external-service actions. They emit their assumptions with their JSON results. The 20 model tests cover known answers, boundaries and important failure cases. The original execution demo remains a single-process local teaching implementation. Neither set of tests establishes production security or a real model's competence.

The two scientific plots are generated from the analytical functions using matplotlib 3.9.2. Three additional SVGs are conceptual diagrams. The reading edition is generated from the chapter sources with Markdown 3.7. No external fonts, scripts or analytics are required to read it.

Forecasts were written on 2026-11-01 with qualitative, uncalibrated confidence. Their event thresholds are editorial choices, not statistically estimated probabilities. They share assumptions and must not be combined as independent odds. Resolution requires archived public evidence or a documented search; unavailable evidence can leave a forecast unresolved.

Live comparative benchmarks, recruitment, production integration, adoption and revenue measurements remain unperformed. The protocols identify what evidence would be needed. Publication volume and pull-request counts are not research-quality measurements.
