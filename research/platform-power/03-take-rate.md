# Take rate is not the same as economic power

*Platform power companion · 4 November 2026 · AI-assisted analytical working note.*

A transaction fee is visible; the platform's influence over demand can be less visible. Ranking, access to users and the ability to move a customer relationship elsewhere may matter more to a supplier than a single commission percentage.

**Accounting model.** Let contribution per completed transaction be revenue minus payment expense, fulfillment expense, support, expected fraud loss and platform charges. Acquisition spending must be deducted separately. A lower headline fee can still leave a merchant worse off if paid placement becomes necessary. Conversely, a fee may be worthwhile if it brings genuinely incremental demand and reduces support costs.

Apple's public ecosystem estimate explicitly distinguishes commerce facilitated from commission-bearing commerce. Use that distinction when comparing businesses: gross merchandise value, net revenue, gross profit and supplier surplus are different quantities. Summing them produces a misleading portrait of platform strength.

An agent marketplace should disclose whether recommendations are influenced by compensation. A sponsored option can remain useful, but a user optimizing for price should not unknowingly receive a ranking optimized for the platform's margin.

**Proposed measurement.** Follow a supplier cohort before and after a fee or ranking change, tracking net margin, repeat customers, paid acquisition and off-platform alternatives. Report selection effects: the suppliers who leave may be absent from platform dashboards.

**Falsifier.** If the platform creates no incremental demand or operational saving, a persistent fee premium requires an explanation such as switching costs rather than a claim of superior coordination.
