# Reputation is evidence with a domain

*Platform power companion · 4 November 2026 · AI-assisted analytical working note.*

A restaurant's fulfillment history, a developer's software reliability and a user's payment behavior are different kinds of evidence. Collapsing them into one global number can create false confidence and unfair spillovers.

**Proposed representation.** Reputation records should state the evaluated operation, observation period, sample size, verification method and dispute status. A provider with ten successful low-value bookings has not demonstrated competence for high-value financial transfers. Absence of recorded failure may simply mean little usage.

An identity identifier can link records, but it cannot make the records true. DID Core's verification architecture is a useful distinction: authenticating control of an identifier is not equivalent to validating every associated assertion.

An agent marketplace needs resistance to fabricated transactions, reciprocal reviews and selective reporting. It also needs an appeals process for erroneous evidence. Otherwise a concentrated discovery system can amplify a single mistaken reputation judgment across a user's economic life.

**Proposed evaluation.** Use synthetic fraudulent and legitimate activity to test detection while measuring false exclusions. Assess whether reputation predicts future task outcomes after accounting for supplier tenure and transaction type.

**Failure condition.** If the score mainly tracks incumbency or advertising exposure, it may reinforce concentration without improving matching. Publicly report calibration by task category and give users access to the evidence behind consequential decisions.

The goal is portable, interpretable evidence where appropriate—not a universal social score.
