# Open participation can strengthen the platform

*Platform power companion · 4 November 2026 · AI-assisted analytical working note.*

A marketplace faces a strategic choice between extracting from trapped participants and becoming the easiest place for willing participants to transact. Openness can expand useful supply and lower adoption resistance, even if it reduces some switching barriers.

**Proposal.** Publish capability schemas, support multiple clients, allow provider multihoming and offer usable exports. Compete on matching quality, execution reliability, dispute resolution and user trust. Openness is meaningful only if a supplier can actually receive business and a user can actually move an active workflow.

The DMA anti-steering decision against Apple in April 2025 illustrates that restrictions on alternative routes can become a regulatory issue. This note uses that historical event as a strategic constraint, not as legal advice or a forecast of future enforcement.

A platform can still earn revenue for useful services: discovery, hosting, verification, payments coordination or operational support. The research question is whether those charges correspond to value participants cannot obtain as efficiently elsewhere.

**Proposed experiment.** Compare participation and retention before and after introducing export and multihoming support. Track both departures and new adoption. A short-term increase in exits may coexist with a longer-term improvement in trust.

**Falsifier.** If openness produces unacceptable fraud, fragmented support or unsustainable costs, the design needs stronger interfaces and governance. The conclusion should not jump directly from one bad implementation to the claim that closed ecosystems are inevitable.
