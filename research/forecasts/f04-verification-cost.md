# F04 · Verification remains a material operating cost

**Issued:** 2026-11-01 · **Resolve by:** 2028-12-31 · **Status:** open
**Working confidence:** medium, qualitative and uncalibrated. These are editorial hypotheses, not measured probabilities or promises.

## Event

At least two independent public workflow evaluations report verification plus human recovery as at least 10% of measured total task cost in an effectful workflow, after including failed attempts.

## Mechanism

Cheaper generation can expose the cost of establishing whether the intended effect occurred.

## Qualification boundary

Use actual measured cost accounting, not hypothetical token prices. State whether human time is monetized and provide the conversion.

## Resolution procedure

Require task-level data or a transparent decomposition. Do not generalize the finding to all software. Search the named product documentation where applicable, scholarly indexes and public repositories. Save the evidence and search date. At the deadline mark true if qualifying public evidence exists, false only after a documented search finds none, and unresolved if access or interpretation prevents adjudication. Keep the original wording when updating status.

## Evidence against the mechanism

If comparable studies consistently show negligible checking and recovery cost at high reliability, the prediction weakens.

The event is deliberately narrower than the mechanism. Its occurrence would not establish market-wide adoption, causality or Walify's success. Forecasts share assumptions and should not be multiplied as independent probabilities.
