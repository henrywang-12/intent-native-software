# F06 · Open weights support more than prototype deployments

**Issued:** 2026-11-01 · **Resolve by:** 2028-12-31 · **Status:** open
**Working confidence:** medium, qualitative and uncalibrated. These are editorial hypotheses, not measured probabilities or promises.

## Event

At least two independently operated public projects publish reproducible effectful-agent evaluations using downloadable model weights, including authorization failures and recovery outcomes.

## Mechanism

Deployable model alternatives should broaden the operator base, even without universal frontier parity.

## Qualification boundary

Weights must be available under stated terms at resolution; do not infer OSI conformity. A chat-only benchmark is insufficient.

## Resolution procedure

Archive model revision, serving configuration, task set and result files. Require a documented reproduction separate from the project author. Search the named product documentation where applicable, scholarly indexes and public repositories. Save the evidence and search date. At the deadline mark true if qualifying public evidence exists, false only after a documented search finds none, and unresolved if access or interpretation prevents adjudication. Keep the original wording when updating status.

## Evidence against the mechanism

If only unreproducible demonstrations qualify, the prediction remains unmet.

The event is deliberately narrower than the mechanism. Its occurrence would not establish market-wide adoption, causality or Walify's success. Forecasts share assumptions and should not be multiplied as independent probabilities.
