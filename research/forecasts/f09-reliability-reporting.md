# F09 · Completion reporting includes unauthorized effects

**Issued:** 2026-11-01 · **Resolve by:** 2028-12-31 · **Status:** open
**Working confidence:** high, qualitative and uncalibrated. These are editorial hypotheses, not measured probabilities or promises.

## Event

At least three new or substantially revised public agent evaluations released after 2026-11-01 report both legitimate task completion and unintended or policy-violating effects with explicit denominators.

## Mechanism

Outcome quality becomes harder to assess through a single success score as agents act across services.

## Qualification boundary

Require stateful effectful tasks and separate utility and violation metrics. Existing unchanged suites do not qualify.

## Resolution procedure

Record release dates, evaluation code and the exact metric definitions. Count independent evaluation projects, not repeated model runs. Search the named product documentation where applicable, scholarly indexes and public repositories. Save the evidence and search date. At the deadline mark true if qualifying public evidence exists, false only after a documented search finds none, and unresolved if access or interpretation prevents adjudication. Keep the original wording when updating status.

## Evidence against the mechanism

If major new evaluations continue reporting only aggregate success, confidence falls.

The event is deliberately narrower than the mechanism. Its occurrence would not establish market-wide adoption, causality or Walify's success. Forecasts share assumptions and should not be multiplied as independent probabilities.
