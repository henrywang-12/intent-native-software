# F05 · Interoperability lowers second-host integration effort

**Issued:** 2026-11-01 · **Resolve by:** 2029-12-31 · **Status:** open
**Working confidence:** medium, qualitative and uncalibrated. These are editorial hypotheses, not measured probabilities or promises.

## Event

A public provider-integration study spanning at least five capabilities reports lower median lifecycle effort for a second compatible host than a second incompatible host at matched acceptance quality.

## Mechanism

Shared semantics and authorization conventions should reduce repeated provider work.

## Qualification boundary

Lifecycle effort must include at least one version-change exercise and support setup. Code-line counts alone do not qualify.

## Resolution procedure

Require independent task assignment or transparent matching and uncertainty estimates. Five is a scope floor, not a claim of statistical power. Search the named product documentation where applicable, scholarly indexes and public repositories. Save the evidence and search date. ## Evidence against the mechanism

If standards move cost into bespoke policy and support work without net savings, the prediction fails.

The event is deliberately narrower than the mechanism. Its occurrence would not establish market-wide adoption, causality or Walify's success. Forecasts share assumptions and should not be multiplied as independent probabilities.
