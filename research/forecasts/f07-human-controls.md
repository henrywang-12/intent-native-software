# F07 · Agent products preserve conventional task controls

**Issued:** 2026-11-01 · **Resolve by:** 2028-12-31 · **Status:** open
**Working confidence:** medium, qualitative and uncalibrated. These are editorial hypotheses, not measured probabilities or promises.

## Event

At least three of ChatGPT, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, Tencent Yuanbao and Alibaba Qwen publicly document a user-visible way to inspect and stop future execution of a multi-step task, beyond ending the current chat.

## Mechanism

Persistent delegation creates demand for controls over ongoing commitments.

## Qualification boundary

Resolve against public documentation and an accessible product demonstration. Do not count an emergency account deletion path.

## Resolution procedure

The panel is frozen as named here. Product renaming with continuity counts; discontinued products stay in the denominator. Search the named product documentation where applicable, scholarly indexes and public repositories. Save the evidence and search date. At the deadline mark true if qualifying public evidence exists, false only after a documented search finds none, and unresolved if access or interpretation prevents adjudication. Keep the original wording when updating status.

## Evidence against the mechanism

If control remains limited to interrupting text generation, this prediction is unmet.

The event is deliberately narrower than the mechanism. Its occurrence would not establish market-wide adoption, causality or Walify's success. Forecasts share assumptions and should not be multiplied as independent probabilities.
