# F03 · Typed capabilities beat GUI automation on bounded tasks

**Issued:** 2026-11-01 · **Resolve by:** 2028-12-31 · **Status:** open
**Working confidence:** medium, qualitative and uncalibrated. These are editorial hypotheses, not measured probabilities or promises.

## Event

A public matched-planner study reports a statistically supported improvement in authorized verified completion for typed capabilities over GUI automation across at least three task families, with no increase in prohibited effects.

## Mechanism

Reducing ambiguous interface steps should help where the provider can expose stable semantics.

## Qualification boundary

Require held-out tasks, comparable total budgets, coverage reporting and confidence intervals. A vendor demo or unmatched leaderboard comparison does not qualify.

## Resolution procedure

Resolve using a published study and available task/evaluation artifacts. Count effect violations separately from success. Search the named product documentation where applicable, scholarly indexes and public repositories. Save the evidence and search date. At the deadline mark true if qualifying public evidence exists, false only after a documented search finds none, and unresolved if access or interpretation prevents adjudication. Keep the original wording when updating status.

## Evidence against the mechanism

A well-powered matched comparison finding no meaningful advantage would lower confidence even before the deadline.

The event is deliberately narrower than the mechanism. Its occurrence would not establish market-wide adoption, causality or Walify's success. Forecasts share assumptions and should not be multiplied as independent probabilities.
