# F08 · Interoperability does not eliminate gateways

**Issued:** 2026-11-01 · **Resolve by:** 2029-12-31 · **Status:** open
**Working confidence:** medium, qualitative and uncalibrated. These are editorial hypotheses, not measured probabilities or promises.

## Event

At least two independent public service providers document distributing the same capability through two or more distinct assistant hosts while those hosts retain separate discovery or ranking interfaces.

## Mechanism

Provider multihoming and concentrated discovery can coexist; openness at the contract layer does not imply one neutral user interface.

## Qualification boundary

Require actual public integrations with distinct operators, not a roadmap or two models inside one host.

## Resolution procedure

Archive provider documentation and host listings. This is a structural prediction, not a forecast of market share or monopoly. Search the named product documentation where applicable, scholarly indexes and public repositories. Save the evidence and search date. At the deadline mark true if qualifying public evidence exists, false only after a documented search finds none, and unresolved if access or interpretation prevents adjudication. Keep the original wording when updating status.

## Evidence against the mechanism

If integrations converge on a single neutral discovery surface instead, the proposed coexistence mechanism is weakened.

The event is deliberately narrower than the mechanism. Its occurrence would not establish market-wide adoption, causality or Walify's success. Forecasts share assumptions and should not be multiplied as independent probabilities.
