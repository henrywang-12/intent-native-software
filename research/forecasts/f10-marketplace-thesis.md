# F10 · The monopoly claim remains unproven without participation data

**Issued:** 2026-11-01 · **Resolve by:** 2030-12-31 · **Status:** open
**Working confidence:** low, qualitative and uncalibrated. These are editorial hypotheses, not measured probabilities or promises.

## Event

A public empirical study of an agent-service marketplace estimates cross-side participation effects and tests at least one counterforce such as multihoming, portability or provider differentiation.

## Mechanism

The platform-power debate should move from analogy to measurable participation mechanisms as markets mature.

## Qualification boundary

A simulation with unestimated coefficients, a market-size slide or a single-company growth curve does not qualify.

## Resolution procedure

Require a transparent identification strategy and explicit limitations; a credible null result counts as the predicted research event. Search the named product documentation where applicable, scholarly indexes and public repositories. Save the evidence and search date. At the deadline mark true if qualifying public evidence exists, false only after a documented search finds none, and unresolved if access or interpretation prevents adjudication. Keep the original wording when updating status.

## Evidence against the mechanism

If discussion remains analogical with no identifiable participation evidence, the research-maturation prediction fails.

The event is deliberately narrower than the mechanism. Its occurrence would not establish market-wide adoption, causality or Walify's success. Forecasts share assumptions and should not be multiplied as independent probabilities.
