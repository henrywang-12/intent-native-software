# P06 · Does compensation preserve its own authority?

**Status: proposed protocol; not run.** The local analytical models are separate artifacts.

## Estimand

Evaluate partial completion of a multi-provider workflow and the subsequent recovery proposal.

## Design and control

Fail one step after an earlier commitment. Vary cancellation fees, nonrefundable components and whether the user authorized compensation.

## Outcomes

Measure residual loss, extra unauthorized effects, comprehension of remaining obligations and time to a stable final state.

## Confounds and boundaries

A refund is not equivalent to the original action never occurring. Include information disclosures and deadlines that cannot be undone.

## Decision and reporting rule

Require a distinct authorized compensation path and an explicit residual-effects report. Compare against manual recovery rather than an impossible perfect rollback.

Record assignment, exclusions, all attempts, versions and budgets. Choose sample size and a stopping rule before confirmatory data collection; publish null and adverse results. Live-service or participant studies require an appropriate separate study setup.

**Methodological context:** [S09: Sagas](https://sigmodrecord.org/1987/12/09/sagas/); [S26: The Ethics of Advanced AI Assistants](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.16244v2). These sources motivate the design; they did not run this proposed study.
