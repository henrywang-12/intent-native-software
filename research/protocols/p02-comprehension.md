# P02 · Can users understand delegated commitments?

**Status: proposed protocol; not run.** The local analytical models are separate artifacts.

## Estimand

Measure whether the review interface improves understanding of recipients, effects, cost and remaining authority.

## Design and control

Randomize consenting participants between a concise structured review, a conversational review and a conventional form. Counterbalance scenario order and accessibility modes.

## Outcomes

Score an unprompted explanation before execution, then ask targeted questions. Track mistaken approvals and completion time separately.

## Confounds and boundaries

Use synthetic consequential actions. Mask the study hypothesis; account for prior AI experience and reading proficiency. Click-through is not comprehension.

## Decision and reporting rule

Prefer a design only if comprehension improves without unacceptable access barriers. Determine sample size from a pilot variance estimate before the confirmatory study.

Record assignment, exclusions, all attempts, versions and budgets. Choose sample size and a stopping rule before confirmatory data collection; publish null and adverse results. Live-service or participant studies require an appropriate separate study setup.
