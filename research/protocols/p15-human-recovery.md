# P15 · Does assistance preserve recovery competence?

**Status: proposed protocol; not run.** The local analytical models are separate artifacts.

## Estimand

Measure whether people can take over a failed workflow after repeated agent use.

## Design and control

In a consented longitudinal simulation, randomize assisted and manual practice, then introduce comparable failures with identical state summaries.

## Outcomes

Track recovery correctness, time, misunderstanding and confidence calibration across sessions.

## Confounds and boundaries

Separate learning effects from fatigue and task familiarity. Do not expose participants to real financial or interpersonal consequences.

## Decision and reporting rule

A convenience gain is incomplete if recovery ability deteriorates substantially. Prespecify a meaningful loss margin from pilot and domain requirements.

Record assignment, exclusions, all attempts, versions and budgets. Choose sample size and a stopping rule before confirmatory data collection; publish null and adverse results. Live-service or participant studies require an appropriate separate study setup.
