# P10 · Do contracts reduce maintenance cost?

**Status: proposed protocol; not run.** The local analytical models are separate artifacts.

## Estimand

Estimate engineering effort per maintained successful workflow over repeated provider changes.

## Design and control

Assign comparable integration tasks to conventional bespoke adapters and shared contracts. Introduce schema, policy and semantic changes on a predetermined schedule.

## Outcomes

Measure implementation, review, debugging and support time; track regressions and the fraction of workflows still correct.

## Confounds and boundaries

Record opt-outs, unfinished work and concurrent-agent use. Developer familiarity and unequal tooling can confound the comparison.

## Decision and reporting rule

Prefer the contract approach only when lifecycle effort improves at matched quality. Freeze analysis and power assumptions after a pilot, before confirmatory observations.

Record assignment, exclusions, all attempts, versions and budgets. Choose sample size and a stopping rule before confirmatory data collection; publish null and adverse results. Live-service or participant studies require an appropriate separate study setup.
