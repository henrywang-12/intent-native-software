# Research edition · 2026-11-01

The edition adds a continuous position paper, evidence register, proposed studies, forecast ledger, executable analytical models and a standalone reading artifact. It was prepared as 100 focused contributions: 30 evidence assessments, 25 chapters, 20 protocols, 10 forecasts, five model packages, five figures and five publication/validation packages. Contribution count is workflow organization, not a measure of scientific quality.

## Validation scope

The local verification commands are listed in the root README. The baseline demo has 24 tests; the new analytical models have 20 tests; publication utilities have six tests. The structural validator checks source-note correspondence, local links, forecast fields, manuscript freshness and HTML anchors. `--check-render` also compares the HTML to a fresh build and requires Markdown 3.7.

The model outputs in [analytical-results.json](analytical-results.json) are generated by `python3 scripts/run_models.py`; they are deterministic hypothetical results, not measurements. The original demo's nine recovery scenarios remain separate.

The Research reproducibility workflow runs on Python 3.10 and 3.13 with read-only repository permissions. A workflow definition is not evidence of a completed remote run; inspect GitHub Actions for the status of the commit being reviewed.

## Editorial review

A fresh AI reader distinguished the thesis, implemented work, ecosystem evidence and conditional monopoly mechanism. Revisions clarified the commitment boundary for revocation, value creation versus capture, execution versus satisfaction, three separate falsifiable claims, a worked recovery example and concrete forecast events. This was an editorial check, not peer review or an external endorsement.

## Operating boundaries

All implementation work for this edition was isolated to this public research repository. No private Walify product files were used or changed. The paper describes an author-stated company vision and the public demo separately. No human study, production benchmark, real payment or third-party service execution was performed.
